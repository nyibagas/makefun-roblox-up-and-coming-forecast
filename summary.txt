Model summary

For each place k in 1..10, every candidate game (rank <= 35 on the last chart read) gets a weight. The weight is a kernel over the distance between k and the game's current rank: a Gaussian whose spread grows with the forecast hour t (sd = 1.6 + 0.2*sqrt(t)). It is mixed with a flat tail (EPS = 0.22, spread over 35 candidates) so lower-ranked games keep some probability. The weights are averaged over the 72 hourly slots with a mild decay (max(0.02, 1 - 0.003*t)). Each place's weights are normalized and scaled by a per-place share, so the probabilities for place k sum to at most SHARE[k-1]. Rows with p < 5e-5 are dropped. top10 is the first 10 games of the current chart. The output is deterministic and uses only the standard library.

Per-place shares: SHARE = [0.70, 0.70, 0.99, 0.99, 0.99, 0.90, 0.70, 0.80, 0.99, 0.70]. This replaced the single constant S = 0.88 from the original spec. The values were given to me in a later instruction, and I did not derive them. They look tuned against the practice data, which has only 10 reads, so the improvement may not carry over to the real window.
